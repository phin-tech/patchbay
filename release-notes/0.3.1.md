## LiteLLM

- **Subscribe to a LiteLLM proxy's MCP servers.** Servers ▸ Subscribe to LiteLLM… takes the proxy's URL and your
  LiteLLM key, and lists the servers your key can use there before you keep them. Each one is reached through LiteLLM,
  which runs it and holds its keys, so nothing it lists runs on your Mac. Your key stays in your Keychain.
- **Changes in LiteLLM wait for you**, like a team's: a server added or taken out there shows above your servers, and
  nothing changes until you accept it.
- **Tools keep their own names.** LiteLLM puts its server's name before each tool's; Patchbay takes it off, so your AI
  tools see `github_mcp__search_issues`, not `github_mcp__GitHub_MCP-search_issues`. Any server can ask for this with
  `tool_prefix` in `patchbay.toml`.
- From a terminal: `patchbay teams litellm <url>`.

## Settings

- **Settings ▸ Teams is now Subscriptions**, listing your teams and LiteLLMs together, with a field to replace a
  LiteLLM's key.
